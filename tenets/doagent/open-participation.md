# Tenet: Open Participation

## Tenet

**Description**: Agents should be able to join, leave, and contribute resources in a transparent way. The system should favor open protocols, explicit capabilities, and clear contracts so that participation scales across organisations and infrastructures.

**Quote**: *"Membership is a capability, not a permission slip."*

**Examples**:
- Agents advertise capabilities and resource limits through a shared registry.
- New agents can discover shared data endpoints and participate safely.
- External systems can integrate through stable, documented interfaces.

**Counter-examples**:
- Hard coded agent lists that require redeployments to change.
- Participation that depends on private knowledge of internal endpoints.
- Protocols that are proprietary or require closed tooling.

**Conflicts**:
- Potential conflict with strict governance and safety requirements.
- Resolution: enforce admission and policy checks while keeping interfaces open.
